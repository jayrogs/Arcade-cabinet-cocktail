Music slots, matching the NES:

  title       title screen and high score page
  select      mode select screen
  fever       in-game music when FEVER is chosen (also the attract demo)
  chill       in-game music when CHILL is chosen
  feverclear  stage clear jingle (Fever)
  chillclear  stage clear jingle (Chill)
  gameover    bottle overflowed, or the computer took the match
  victory     a versus round won (match still going)
  matchwin    a human wins the versus match

Drop .ogg, .mp3 or .wav files here. A file named exactly after a slot (fever.mp3) wins.
Otherwise the filename is matched on words: "fever", "chill", "title", "select"/"mode"/"menu",
"fever clear", "chill clear", "game over", "vs"/"versus"/"match" for matchwin, "victory" or
"round" for the round jingle. Anything not supplied keeps the built-in chiptune for that slot.
